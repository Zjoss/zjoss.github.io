About the app

Course materials
and prices, in one place.

Heavens Door helps you work with training programs: organize course descriptions and terms, check prices, and prepare proposals for customers.

How we handle data ↗
01

Course information

Keep descriptions, dates, terms, and working notes together in one workspace.

02

Google Sheets pricing

An optional connection reads prices from sources you configure. Manually entered prices are stored separately.

03

Customer proposals

Use your materials to prepare emails and proposals, with an optional AI assistant you configure separately.

Google connection

Read prices without changing spreadsheets

In the local Windows version, you sign in to Google and choose the spreadsheets, sheets, and cells or lookup rules to use. The app reads values and metadata for the selected spreadsheets through the Google Sheets API.

The spreadsheets.readonly permission technically allows the app to read all spreadsheets available to the connected account. The app reads the sources you configure; this connection does not request permission to write, edit, or delete spreadsheets.

Connecting Google is optional: you can use your own prices. You can disconnect the app and revoke access in your Google Account settings.
